## Part 4: The Teleportation Portal & The Mask Clue
Deep within the lowest level of the castle's central keep is a chamber entirely removed from the swamp's muck—a pristine, ancient elven teleportation circle.

### Read-Aloud Text
> Beneath the rotting wood and wet stone of the castle lies a vaulted cellar made of smooth, unweathered white marble. In the center of the room, a massive circular ring of silver runes glows with a cold, pale blue light. The air here tastes like mountain air; crisp, thin, and frozen. On a stone pedestal next to the gate sits a heavy leather ledger left behind by Wyrmspeaker Rezmir. The final entry reads: "The thieves have slipped into the marsh with the Black Mask. The Hoard is secure at the lodge, but my position is compromised if I do not return with the relic. I am stepping through the gate to secure the perimeter on the other side. Speak the phrase 'Drako' while stepping into the ring to trace the link."
