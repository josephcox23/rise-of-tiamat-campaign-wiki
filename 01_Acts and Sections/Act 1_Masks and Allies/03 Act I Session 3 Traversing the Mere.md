# Act I, Session 3: Traversing the Mere

## DM Objective
Integrate the High Road faction encounters at the border of the marsh, resolve the Sunken Gate Puzzle to reveal Logan's weapon seed, navigate the treacherous inner swamp using newly acquired boons, and establish the tactical setup for entering the castle via the lizardfolk scouts.

- *The Waystone Inn* (Harper connection)
- Fleeing clerics (Order of the Gauntlet connection)
- Zhentarim Cache (hidden)
- Sunken Gate Puzzle (Verdant Fang info)
- Travel in the marsh
	- meet Snapjaw, persuade to help
---

## Part 1: The Last Stop on the High Road

Before the party steps off the established highway and into the lawless muck of the Mere of Dead Men, they cross paths with the final remnants of civilization and faction networks operating on the border.

### The Harper Connection
* **The Encounter:** At *The Waystone*, a crowded coaching inn sitting right on the edge of the high road before the swamp trail begins. A half-elf bard sits near the hearth, performing a specific, subtle melodic theme on a lute.
* **The Interaction:** When Jacob (the Bard) listens closely, mirrors, or musically interacts with the tune, the agent catches his eye and discreetly reveals themselves.
* **SUCCESS - Reward Unlocked:** The agent quietly passes over the **[[Harper Cipher Ring]]** and **2 doses of [[Dust of Disappearance]]**. They give a crucial warning: *"Rezmir knows someone stole her prize in Waterdeep. The cult has structural checkpoints deeper in, and she's using the local bullywugs to hunt anything that moves."*

### The Order of the Gauntlet Drop
* **The Encounter:** Pushing north from the inn toward the coordinates of the secret route, the party crosses paths with a battered, mud-splattered wagon driven by wounded clerics of Torm fleeing a razed chapel located on the swamp's fringe.
* **The Interaction:** If Andy (the Ranger) or a martial character steps forward to offer aid or swears a solemn oath to avenge the chapel, the paladins recognize their light.
* **SUCCESS - Reward Unlocked:** They gift a holy heirloom salvaged from the altar: **The [[Lion's Battle Horn]]**. *(Bonus action: Allies within 30 feet gain 10 temporary HP and immunity to the Frightened condition for 1 minute; 1/long rest).*

---

## Part 2: The Sunken Gate Puzzle & The Hidden Cache

Following the directions from the cultist dispatch letter, the party descends a steep, rocky ravine where the marsh water pools over ancient ruins.

### Exploration Side Objective: The Zhentarim Cache
* **The Discovery:** As the party works their way down the wet rock face toward the pool, a keen eye catches a faint, dark smudge on the stone—the signature winged snake of the Black Network carved into a hidden nook.
* **The Action:** A character can use an action to make a **DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or Sleight of Hand check** to safely scale down the slick rock face and retrieve the concealed box.
* **SUCCESS - Reward Unlocked:** Cache 1 contains **2 [[Potion of Invisibility| Potions of Invisibility]]** and a tattered piece of parchment outlining the physical coordinates of the remaining two caches deeper inside the Mere.

### The Sunken Gate
> At the bottom of the ravine, the brackish swamp water turns crystal clear in a secluded pool, revealing an ancient, submerged elven archway of green-veined marble sealed by a heavy stone slab. Three stone pillars rise out of the pool, each topped with an obsidian dragon's head clamped tightly around a glowing emerald gemstone. 
> 
> Carved into the green marble archway in Elvish and Druidic runes is a riddle: *"The Emerald Queen demands her tribute. Feed the teeth that do not bite, heal the rot that brings the night, and speak the secret word of life."*

#### Resolving the Pillars
To lower the stone slab and unlock the underwater passage into the cult's secret routes, the players must clear the three dragon pillars:

1. **Pillar of the Teeth (Martial Check):** The first dragon head has razor-sharp stone fangs, but its throat is clogged with petrified wood. A character must make a **DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Sleight of Hand check** to clear the blockage without shattering the gemstone inside.
2. **Pillar of the Rot (Logan/Druid Check):** The second dragon head is weeping black, corrupted swamp sludge from its eyes. Logan can use a spell slot of 1st level or higher to cast a healing/purification spell, or make a **DC 15 Wisdom (Nature) check** to scrub the rot away using the Mire-Root Salve from their Emerald Enclave boon.
3. **Pillar of the Secret Word (Jacob/Bard Check):** The third dragon head hums with an ancient, silent magical frequency. Jacob must make a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check** to recognize the melody of an ancient elven song of regrowth, or strum a chord on his instrument that resonates with the stone structure.

* **Failure Consequence:** If a check fails by 5 or more, the gemstone flashes with acid energy. The rolling character must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**, taking `3d6` acid damage on a failure (half as much on a success) before the pillar resets.

### The Lore Drop: Verdant Fang Awakening
When all three pillars are resolved, the emeralds flare with brilliant green light, the jaws snap open, and the heavy stone slab grinds downward into the earth to open the path. 

As the gate clears, a sudden, powerful vision flashes directly into Logan’s mind:
> You see a great green dragon from a bygone age falling out of the sky, its heart pierced by a brilliant emerald spear. As the spear shatters, its splintered shaft sinks into the muck and twisting roots of a massive, ancient banyan tree deep within this very swamp. The voice of the wild whispers in your mind: The Verdant Fang is waiting for a worthy hand to pull it free.

## Part 3: Navigating the Deeps

With the gate open, the party traverses the secret underwater tunnel, venting up into the dense, hazardous inner marsh.
* **Swamp Navigation:** Moving safely through the shifting mud paths requires a **DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check**.
  * *Faction Synergy:* If the party utilizes their Emerald Enclave Map or Zhentarim coordinates, the check automatically succeeds.
  * *Failure:* The journey takes twice as long, and the grueling, toxic bog inflicts 1 collective level of Exhaustion on the party.

---

### Read-Aloud Text
> The narrow mud trail you are following eventually opens into a wide, humid clearing. Crouched low in the reeds ahead is a hunting party of lizardfolk, their weapons drawn but held in a tense, waiting stance. Their leader, a heavily scarred hunter named Snapjaw, steps forward. His slitted eyes dart from your armor directly to the Black Dragon Mask carried by your group. He speaks in broken, hissing Draconic: "You carry the face of the swamp-tyrant, stone-walkers. The half-dragon Rezmir has turned our home upside down searching for it. She tears our skin and feeds our youth to the bullywugs because her prize was stolen in the city. Do you bring it back to beg her mercy, or do you come to help us break her chains?"

### Roleplay & Social Interaction
The lizardfolk are being brutally punished by [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]]'s bullywugs because [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] is taking her fury out on them over the lost mask. They want a rebellion, but seeing the mask makes them terrified that the party might just be cult executioners coming to finish them off.

* **The Check:** **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check** to win [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw|Snapjaw’s]] trust. Wielding their ancestral weapons or promising to use the mask as bait to draw Rezmir into an ambush grants Advantage on this check.
* **SUCCESS - Snapjaw's Alliance:** Snapjaw agrees to smuggle the party into Castle Naerytar via a hidden drainage grate, bypassing the outer boardwalk defenses entirely. He views the party's mask as the ultimate tool to spark a full-scale slave revolt.
* **FAILURE - Alert Status:** Terrified of Rezmir's retribution, Snapjaw's party retreats into the fog to warn the stronghold. The castle goes on standard high alert, forcing the party to face a fully prepared front-gate defense.

# The Infiltration of Naerytar

![[Castle Naerytar.jpeg]]

## DM Objective
Navigate the castle's internal fractures, eliminate or bypass the local commanders, use the Black Dragon Mask to control or disrupt the castle's defenses, and uncover the portal leading out of the swamp before Rezmir's hunters close the trap.

---

## Part 1: Entering the Stronghold
Depending on the choices made in Section 3, the party enters through one of two primary entry points.

### Entry Point Options

#### Option A: The Front Gate Boardwalks (No Alliance)
* The Situation: If the lizardfolk were alerted in the outskirts, Rezmir knows the thief is at the gates. The entryway is reinforced.
* The Opposition: 4x Cultists and 2x Guard Drakes patrol this zone constantly.
* The Complication: Sneaking requires a group DC 16 Dexterity (Stealth) check. Failure rings the courtyard alarm bell, putting the entire fortress on high alert (+2 to all enemy passive perception checks inside the castle).

```encounter
  - John (Jacob)

  - Logan

  - "1": Cult Fanatic
  - "1": Cult Fanatic
  - "1": Cult Fanatic
  - "1": Cult Fanatic
  - "1": Guard Drake
  - "1": Guard Drake
```


#### Option B: The Western Drainage Grate (Snapjaw's Alliance)
* The Situation: If [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw|Snapjaw]] was befriended, the party uses a partially submerged iron grate hidden by thick reeds.
* The Advantage: The party has Advantage on stealth checks to enter. Snapjaw's scouts have intentionally left the wooden bar unlatched from the inside.
* The Entryway: Leads directly into the wet cellars underneath the central keep, bypassing the courtyard patrols entirely.

---

## Part 2: The Three-Way Powder Keg
The interior of Camp Naerytar is a tense political landscape. The cultists are in charge, but their underlings despise each other. Smart players can exploit these fractures.

* The Cultists: Led by [[06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] and [[Dralmorrer Borngray]]. Located in the Command Quarters (Top Floors). Agenda: Despise the swamp; just want the hoard moved through the portal.
* The Bullywugs: Led by [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]]. Located in the Mud Caves (Sub-level). Agenda: Cruel zealots who abuse the lizardfolk; heavily favored by Rezmir.
* The Lizardfolk: Led by [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw|Snapjaw]]. Located in the Courtyard Longhouses / Cellars. Agenda: Slaves planning a mutiny; waiting for the party to strike first.

### Triggering the Lizardfolk Rebellion
If the party decides to launch an open attack inside the castle, they can officially trigger the rebellion by eliminating a bullywug commander or displaying a major victory.
* The Catalyst: Killing [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]] in the caves or displaying his head in the courtyard.
* The Effect: The lizardfolk immediately turn their weapons on the bullywugs and lower-level cultists. This locks down 75 percent of the generic guards in a chaotic riot, allowing the party to pursue the main targets without being overwhelmed by numbers.

---

## Part 3: Confronting the Commanders
To secure the fortress, the party must deal with the two primary leaders currently residing in the upper tower and cellars.

### 1. [[06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]] (The Sub-Level Caves)
* The Setup: A bloated bullywug shaman obsessed with necromancy, hiding in the flooded mud tunnels beneath the keep.
* Combat Style: Uses Spike Growth to slow down the martial characters, then bombards the party with Inflict Wounds and swamp vermin summons.
* SUCCESS - Loot Drop: Defeating Pharblex yields his personal spellbook (containing swamp/nature magic) and a +1 Shield adorned with crocodile scales.

# Session Summary


## Overview
>The party successfully secured crucial faction boons along the dangerous northeastern road before navigating the ancient defenses of the Mere of Dead Men. After forming a tense alliance with the local lizardfolk resistance, the party executed a high-stakes infiltration of Castle Naerytar, striking a massive blow against the cult's hierarchy by eliminating the bullywug leadership.
---

## Key Milestones

### 1. Faction Mobilization & The High Road
* **The Harper's Melody:** At the roadside inn, Jacob successfully recognized a hidden melody played by a Harper spy. By swapping tunes to verify his allegiance, he secured the [[Harper Cipher Ring]] and two doses of [[Dust of Disappearance]].
* **The Order's Blessing:** The next morning, the party encountered fleeing clerics of Bahamut whose chapel at Castle Naerytar had been overrun. After giving the clerics solemn assurances that they would clear the stronghold, the party was gifted an Order of the Gauntlet heirloom: [[Lion's Battle Horn|The Lion’s Battle Horn]].
* **The Black Network Supply:** While carefully navigating a steep ravine along the swamp's border, the party discovered a hidden Zhentarim cache. They recovered two [[Potion of Invisibility|Potions of Invisibility]] and a tattered map marking the coordinates of two remaining supply drops deep within the marsh.

### 2. Opening the Sunken Gate
* Reaching the edge of the Mere of Dead Men, the party's progress was blocked by the ancient **Sunken Gate Puzzle**. Through clever investigation and teamwork, they solved the trial, causing the heavy stone mechanisms to shift and granting them access to the sulfurous waterways of the inner marsh.

### 3. The Lizardfolk Alliance
* Deep in the reeds, the party was surrounded by [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw|Snapjaw]] and his hunting band of lizardfolk warriors.
* **The Intimidation:** Before weapons could clash, [[Logan]] stepped forward and successfully intimidated the hunter boss. 
* Impressed and terrified by the party's resolve, Snapjaw exposed the fragile political crack inside the castle: the lizardfolk are being enslaved by the cult-backed bullywugs. He agreed to help the party under one condition—they must assassinate the cruel bullywug shaman, [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]].

### 4. Infiltration & Execution
* **The Grate:** Snapjaw led the party to a hidden western drainage entrance and left the iron door unlatched.
* **The Stealth Infiltration:** Navigating the waterlogged lower cellars required a series of close-call Stealth checks, but the party successfully bypassed the outer boardwalk patrols without raising the alarm.
* **The Strike:** The boys traced the sounds of chanting straight to the flooded mud caves. They breached the command room, caught the targets completely by surprise, and eliminated Pharblex Spatterdock along with three of his elite lieutenants before they could call for reinforcements.
