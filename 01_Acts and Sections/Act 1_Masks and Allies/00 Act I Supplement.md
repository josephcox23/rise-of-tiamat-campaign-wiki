# Act I Supplement: Encounters and Unique Bosses

## Part 1: High Road & Mere of Dead Men Random Encounters

When the party is traveling the High Road or navigating the outer edges of the Mere of Dead Men, roll a d20 once per day and once per night. An encounter occurs on a result of 16 or higher. Roll a d8 on the table below to determine the encounter.

| d8  | High Road Encounters (Day/Night)                                                                                                                          | Mere of Dead Men Encounters (Inner Swamp)                                                                |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | 2x Scout and 1x Dire Wolf (Cult outriders tracking caravans)                                                                                              | 3x Bullywugs and 1x Giant Toad (Patrol looking for lizardfolk slaves)                                    |
| 2   | A battered merchant wagon with a broken axle. The merchant offers a Potion of Healing if the party helps repair it (DC 12 Athletics or Woodworker tools). | 2x Will-o'-Wisps hovering over a false island of safe-looking ground.                                    |
| 3   | 1x Cult Fanatic and 3x Bandits posing as stranded travelers.                                                                                              | 1x Constrictor Snake dropping from the canopy onto the rear PC.                                          |
| 4   | A heavy, unnatural fog rolls in. Heavy rain reduces visibility to 30 feet and imposes disadvantage on Perception checks for 4 hours.                      | 4x Mud Mephits that explode into sticky mud when killed (DC 11 Dex save or blinded for 1 minute).        |
| 5   | 1x Peryton circling high above, waiting to ambush a lone character or animal companion.                                                                   | 1x Swarm of Insects (Beetles) bursting from a hollow, decaying log.                                      |
| 6   | A deserted camp site containing signs of a struggle and a discarded pouch with 14 gp and a silver ring worth 25 gp.                                       | A patch of Quicksand covering a 10-foot square (DC 12 Survival to spot; DC 12 Athletics to escape).      |
| 7   | 2x Thugs demanding a 20 gp "toll" to pass a narrow rock cut.                                                                                              | 2x Lizardfolk Hunters from Snapjaw's tribe, hiding in the reeds and testing the party's reflexes.        |
| 8   | A majestic, non-hostile Giant Elk tracks the party from the tree line before vanishing into the woods.                                                    | A pristine patch of rare marsh flora. A Druid or Herbalist can harvest it to create 1 dose of antitoxin. |
