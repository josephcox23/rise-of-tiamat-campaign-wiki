# Session Summary
::/pf
::pf
The party traveled north along the High Road toward the Mere of Dead Men. Before reaching the swamp's edge, their path was blocked by a massive chasm with a raging river churning at the bottom. A single rope bridge spanned the gap, anchored to a fortified guard tower held by the Cult of the Dragon. The party engaged and successfully dispatched the cultist forces securing the crossing.

---

## Key Discoveries & Rewards

### The Cultist Dispatch Letter
Following the battle, the party searched the guard tower and uncovered a secure cult lockbox containing a encrypted dispatch letter. 
* **The Message:** The letter details a secret, heavily warded underwater entrance into the marsh used by cult elites to bypass natural hazards. The coordinates pinpoint a secluded, crystal-clear pool right at the threshold of the Mere of Dead Men—known as the **Sunken Gate**.
