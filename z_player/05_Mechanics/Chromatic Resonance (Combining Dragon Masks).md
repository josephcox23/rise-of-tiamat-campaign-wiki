To harness the multi-headed power of Tiamat before her full awakening, a creature attuned to one of the legendary Dragon Masks can attempt to fuse additional masks onto it. This process allows a mortal to bridge the power of up to three individual masks before they permanently consolidate into the artifact known as the *Mask of the Dragon Queen*.

## Core Rules

### Attunement and Limits
* **Primary Anchor**: You choose one mask to act as the primary anchor. You must already be attuned to this mask.
* **Resonance Limit**: You can bind a maximum of two additional masks to the primary anchor, for a total of three masks combined.
* **Slot Cost**: Combining masks does not consume additional personal attunement slots. The secondary and tertiary masks feed off the existing attunement link to the primary anchor.

### The Ritual of Fusion
To bind a new mask to your primary anchor, you must perform a 1-hour ritual during a long rest. 
* At the end of the ritual, you must make a **DC 18 Charisma (Performance or Intimidation) check** to assert your authority over the competing draconic spirits.
* **On a Success**: The masks successfully fuse. The physical visage alters, sprouting multi-colored scales or horns reflecting the new combination. You gain the combined properties outlined below.
* **On a Failure**: The fusion fails, you take `4d10` psychic damage, and you gain one immediate failed save on the *Will of the Mask* curse tracking table.

### Escalated Curse: The Discordant Mind
Combining the masks splinters your psyche with competing draconic desires. While holding a combination of two or three masks, your daily saving throw against the **Will of the Mask** curse undergoes the following modifications:
* **Two Masks Combined**: The daily Wisdom saving throw DC increases to **17**.
* **Three Masks Combined**: The daily Wisdom saving throw DC increases to **19**.
* **Shared Progress**: You do not track separate failed saves for each mask. They feed into a single pool of madness that accelerates your transformation into a thrall of Tiamat.

## Stat and Property Modifications

When masks are successfully combined, their mechanical properties stack or evolve according to the rules below.

### 1. Armor Class (AC) Bonus
The supernatural barriers of the masks reinforce one another, but with diminishing returns as the physical weight grows.
* **1 Mask**: +2 Bonus to AC.
* **2 Masks Combined**: +3 Bonus to AC.
* **3 Masks Combined**: +4 Bonus to AC.

### 2. Elemental Affinities (Resistances)
You gain resistance to the damage types associated with every mask currently fused into the item:
* **Black**: Acid
* **Blue**: Lightning
* **Green**: Poison
* **Red**: Fire
* **White**: Cold

> **Immunity Milestone**: If you successfully fuse **three masks** together, you select one of those three associated elemental damage types. Your resistance to that specific damage type upgrades to total **Immunity**.

### 3. Draconic Sight Evolved
Your senses expand based on the combination:
* **Base Benefit**: You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet) regardless of the combination.
* **Sensory Synergy**: You gain the specialized sight properties of **all** combined masks simultaneously (e.g., combining Black and White allows you to see green acidic residues in dim light *and* ignore visual obscurement caused by heavy snow).

### 4. Compounding Auras
The presence of multiple auras alters your social interactions drastically. 
* You retain **Advantage on Intimidation checks**.
* Your **Disadvantage expands** to encompass all social penalties introduced by the combined masks (e.g., combining Black and Green grants disadvantage on both Persuasion checks with non-evil creatures *and* Animal Handling checks).

| Combination       | Title                | Unique Synergy Feature                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :---------------- | :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Black + Green** | *The Caustic Mire*   | **Corrosive Subversion**: When a creature fails its saving throw against your *Draconic Presence*, its armor is corroded. It suffers a permanent -1 penalty to its non-magical AC until the armor is repaired or replaced. |
| **Black + Red**   | *The Boiling Ash*    | **Vapor Scald**: When you take acid or fire damage, you can use your reaction to project a cloud of scalding steam. Creatures within 5 feet of you take fire damage equal to your character level.                         |
| **Blue + Red**    | *The Thunderstorm*   | **Fulminating Detonation**: Once per turn, when you deal fire or lightning damage to a creature, you can force it to make a DC 14 Strength saving throw. On a failure, it is pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone.        |
| **Blue + White**  | *The Glacial Gale*   | **Numbing Shock**: When a creature takes lightning damage from you, its speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of your next turn as frost and electrical spasms lock its joints.                                      |
| **Green + White** | *The Frozen Bramble* | **Toxic Rime**: Your weapons or spell attacks that deal cold or poison damage leave a freezing residue. The target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.                                                 |

## Three-Mask Combinations (Tri-Resonance Examples)

### The Tyrant’s Apex (Blue + Red + Green)
*This combination represents the ultimate tier of tactical dominance, psychological manipulation, and raw destructive output.*
* **AC Bonus**: +4
* **Affinities**: Resistance to Lightning, Fire, and Poison. *(One can be upgraded to Immunity)*.
* **Combined Senses**: Darkvision (+60 ft.), electromagnetic current perception (30 ft.), and thermal signature perception (30 ft.).
* **Tri-Resonance Feature - Apex Authority**: Your *Draconic Presence* action is amplified. The radius increases to 30 feet. Creatures that fail the DC 14 Wisdom saving throw are both Frightened of you and drop whatever they are holding in their hands.

### The Primal Desolation (Black + White + Red)
*Focuses entirely on survival, raw elemental devastation, and environmental adaptation.*
* **AC Bonus**: +4
* **Affinities**: Resistance to Acid, Cold, and Fire. *(One can be upgraded to Immunity)*.
* **Combined Senses**: Darkvision (+60 ft.), acidic residue tracking, blizzard vision, and thermal signature perception.
* **Tri-Resonance Feature - Chromatic Retribution**: Whenever a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes `1d6` acid, `1d6` cold, or `1d6` fire damage (your choice when the hit occurs).
