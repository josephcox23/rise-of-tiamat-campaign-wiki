description: |
  Crafted from smooth, crystalline lapis lazuli and lined with sweeping sapphire-tinted horn protrusions, this mask hums with a faint, constant static charge. When worn, the ridges along the brow structure elevate slightly, aligning with the bone structure of the host.

  The air entering the nostrils of the mask smells sharply of ozone and impending thunderstorms. Small arcs of blue lightning occasionally dance along the horn crests when the wearer concentrates.

  - - - 

  #### Properties (while attuned):
  - +2 Bonus to AC: The mask’s crystalline reinforcement deflects physical blows with supernatural efficiency.
  - Draconic Sight: You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet). In complete darkness, electromagnetic currents become visible as thin, humming blue lines, highlighting underground mechanical structures, traps, or conductors.
  - Lightning Affinity: You gain resistance to lightning damage.
  - Aura of Command: The sound of your voice carries a booming resonance, like thunder over flat plains. You have advantage on Intimidation checks, but disadvantage on Stealth checks when speaking or casting verbal spells due to the resonant echo.
  - Draconic Presence (1/day): As an action, you can exude the mask’s will. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of your next turn. On a success, they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

  #### Will of the Mask (Curse):
  The mask is alive with a fragment of Tiamat’s malice. When you first attune to it, make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the mask subtly entwines its will with yours. Each dawn thereafter, roll a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw against the mask’s influence. The effects are cumulative:
  
  Failed Save 1: 
  - You develop an obsession with order, hierarchy, and personal authority. You demand total compliance from subordinates and become hostile to insubordination.
  
  Failed Save 2: 
  - You begin hearing calculating whispers whenever strategic choices or assets are discussed: Control them. Dictate the terms. Weakness is a choice. You must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom save to avoid taking total, dictatorial command of group decisions.
  
  Failed Save 3: 
  - Your dreams feature massive blue wyrms ruling over vast, dry canyon empires. You gain disadvantage on saving throws against charm or domination effects from dragons or cultists of Tiamat.
  
  Failed Save 4: 
  - The mask fuses to your skin. Small blue scales emerge along your neck and shoulders. Your skin feels hot and dry to the touch, and you spark with static energy whenever handled.
  
  Failed Save 5: 
  - You become a thrall of Tiamat's will. The DM may assume control of your character until the curse is broken. Your body begins to transform into a half-draconic form, driven by rigid tyranny and vanity.

  > Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though small patches of dry, hardened skin may remain.

  #### Lore:
  - Designed to reward calculating minds, the Blue Dragon Mask aligns with individuals who seek dominion through flawless planning and unyielding structure.
  - When combined with other masks, its features merge seamlessly into the Mask of the Dragon Queen, aggregating elemental affinities to prepare the gateway for the dragon deity.
