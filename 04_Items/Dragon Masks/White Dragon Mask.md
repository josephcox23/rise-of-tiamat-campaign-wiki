description: |
  Carved from a singular, flawless block of dense dragon-bone ivory and reinforced with rime-covered platinum filaments, this mask bears a smooth, streamlined, and feral draconic shape.

  The bone surface is freezing to the touch. When placed over the face, it draws the moisture from the air, creating a layer of frost that bonds the mask securely to the wearer's cheekbones and brow.

   - - - 

  #### Properties (while attuned):
  - +2 Bonus to AC: An invisible sheath of absolute-zero air hardens around the mask, deflecting incoming attacks.
  - Draconic Sight: You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet). You ignore all visual obscurement caused by heavy snow, blizzards, or ice glare, maintaining perfect visual clarity in arctic conditions.
  - Cold Affinity: You gain resistance to cold damage.
  - Aura of Isolation: Your emotions freeze over, rendering you entirely unbothered by psychological distress. You have advantage on Intimidation checks and are immune to the Charmed condition, but you suffer disadvantage on Performance or Persuasion checks due to your cold, detached demeanor.
  - Draconic Presence (1/day): As an action, you can exude the mask’s will. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of your next turn. On a success, they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

  #### Will of the Mask (Curse):
  The mask is alive with a fragment of Tiamat’s malice. When you first attune to it, make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the mask subtly entwines its will with yours. Each dawn thereafter, roll a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw against the mask’s influence. The effects are cumulative:
  
  Failed Save 1: 
  - You become cold, quiet, and deeply suspicious of everyone around you. You prefer absolute isolation and refuse to share personal thoughts.
  
  Failed Save 2: 
  - Cold, calculating whispers repeat in your ears during planning: Trust no one. Rely only on your own claws. Survival belongs to the solitary hunter. You must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom save to avoid abandoning your companions during social gatherings or camping situations.

  Failed Save 3: 
  - Your dreams feature endless glacial expansions crushing cities under mountain-sized walls of ice. You gain disadvantage on saving throws against charm or domination effects from dragons or cultists of Tiamat.

  Failed Save 4: 
  - The mask fuses to your skull. Your breath instantly freezes moisture on nearby surfaces, and your skin takes on a pale, translucent blue tint.

  Failed Save 5: 
  - You become a thrall of Tiamat's will. The DM may assume control of your character until the curse is broken. Your body begins to transform into a half-draconic form, ruled entirely by animalistic survival instincts and cold malice.

  >Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though a permanent pale, frostbitten coloration remains around your eyes.

  #### Lore:
  - The White Dragon Mask channels the primal, ancient survival instincts of dragonkind, discarding political scheming in favor of pure, unchecked physical dominion.
  - When aligned with the other four chromatic masks, its ice-forged matrix acts as the balancing anchor that stabilizes the final form of the Mask of the Dragon Queen.
