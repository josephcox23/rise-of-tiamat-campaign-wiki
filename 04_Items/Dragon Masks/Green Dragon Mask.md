  Constructed from polished jade plates layered over dense forest briarwood, this mask mirrors the multi-crested, predatory visage of a green dragon. The jade scales possess a wet sheen, as though freshly slicked with dew or venom.

  When secured to a face, the briar-vines along the rim constrict gently, locking into the user's hairline. A faint fragrance of sweet pine and bitter almonds consistently hangs around the wearer.

  - - - 

  #### Properties (while attuned):
  - +2 Bonus to AC: Interlocking jade plating provides a deflective shield that absorbs kinetic force.
  - Draconic Sight: You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet). You can see perfectly through heavy foliage, mist, or non-magical smoke up to a distance of 30 feet, negating disadvantage on perception checks caused by natural concealment.
  - Poison Affinity: You gain resistance to poison damage and have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.
  - Aura of Intrigue: Your movements become completely silent and fluid. You have advantage on Deception and Intimidation checks, but animals become highly unsettled in your presence, granting disadvantage on Animal Handling checks.
  - Draconic Presence (1/day): As an action, you can exude the mask’s will. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of your next turn. On a success, they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

  #### Will of the Mask (Curse):
  The mask is alive with a fragment of Tiamat’s malice. When you first attune to it, make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the mask subtly entwines its will with yours. Each dawn thereafter, roll a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw against the mask’s influence. The effects are cumulative:

  Failed Save 1: 
  - You feel a compulsive urge to lie, conceal your true intentions, and manipulate conversations, even when telling the truth would be beneficial.

  Failed Save 2: 
  - You hear honeyed whispers when interacting with allies: They are using you. Outmaneuver them before they betray you. You must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom save to resist fabricating an elaborate web of lies or keeping vital secrets from your companions.

  Failed Save 3: 
  - Your dreams are plagued by green eyes watching you from thick, suffocating woodland canopies. You gain disadvantage on saving throws against charm or domination effects from dragons or cultists of Tiamat.

  Failed Save 4: 
  - The mask permanently bonds to your facial structure. Your breath becomes poisonous to small flora, causing nearby grass and flowers to wilt within minutes of your presence.

  Failed Save 5: 
  - You become a thrall of Tiamat's will. The DM may assume control of your character until the curse is broken. Your body begins to transform into a half-draconic form, defined by cruel manipulation and toxic deception.

  > Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though a permanent scent of bitter almonds remains.

  #### Lore:
  - The Green Dragon Mask represents the cunning and subversive element of the cult, often worn by the chief orchestrators of political blackmail and subversion.
  - Bringing the green mask together with its counterparts triggers the transformation into the singular Mask of the Dragon Queen, a necessary focal point for the summoning ritual.
