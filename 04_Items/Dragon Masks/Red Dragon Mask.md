description: |
  Forged from hardened ruby-composite scales and rimmed with sweeping, back-curved horns of blackened bone, this mask radiates a tangible, dry warmth. The jawline is fixed in an aggressive, open snarl.

  When donned, the interior mold shifts like cooling magma to seal perfectly against the wearer's skin. The eyes of the mask flicker with an internal ember-like glow that intensifies with the wearer's temper.

  Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though your skin remains permanently sunburnt and warm to the touch.

  - - - 

  #### Properties (while attuned):
  - +2 Bonus to AC: The shimmering heat ripples around the mask break the trajectory of incoming strikes.
  - Draconic Sight: You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet). You gain the ability to perceive thermal signatures out to 30 feet, allowing you to locate invisible creatures or warm-blooded targets behind thin barriers.
  - Fire Affinity: You gain resistance to fire damage.
  - Aura of Tyranny: Your presence demands absolute attention. You have advantage on Intimidation checks, but your volatile temper causes disadvantage on Insight checks as your own emotions blind you to subtle social cues.
  - Draconic Presence (1/day): As an action, you can exude the mask’s will. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of your next turn. On a success, they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

  #### Will of the Mask (Curse):
  The mask is alive with a fragment of Tiamat’s malice. When you first attune to it, make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the mask subtly entwines its will with yours. Each dawn thereafter, roll a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw against the mask’s influence. The effects are cumulative:
  
  Failed Save 1: 
  - You display extreme arrogance and a destructive temper. You view any criticism or disagreement as a direct personal assault.
  
  Failed Save 2: 
  - Loud, roaring whispers echo in your mind during moments of conflict: Crush them. Burn their achievements to ash. Prove your supremacy. You must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom save to resist resolving a disagreement with immediate physical violence or destructive force.
  
  Failed Save 3: 
  - Your dreams consist of ash rain and endless cities burning under waves of fire. You gain disadvantage on saving throws against charm or domination effects from dragons or cultists of Tiamat.

  Failed Save 4: 
  - The mask fuses completely into your flesh. Your hair turns to ash-colored strands, and small wisps of sulfurous smoke escape your nostrils whenever you breathe or speak aggressively.
  
  Failed Save 5: 
  - You become a thrall of Tiamat's will. The DM may assume control of your character until the curse is broken. Your body begins to transform into a half-draconic form, consumed by raw fury and unquenchable greed.

  >Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though a permanent pale, frostbitten coloration remains around your eyes.

  #### Lore:
  - The Red Dragon Mask is the most overtly aggressive of the set, embodying the destructive might and absolute supremacy that the Cult of the Dragon seeks to unleash upon the world.
  - Serving as a cornerstone piece, it channels the core destructive energy required to link the extra-planar gates when forging the Mask of the Dragon Queen.
