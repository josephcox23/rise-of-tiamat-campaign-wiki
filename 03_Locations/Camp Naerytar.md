# 🏰 Camp Naerytar

## 📌 Overview
An ancient, isolated stone fortress built into the deep marshlands of the Mere of Dead Men. Originally constructed by an elven wizard, it has been seized by the Cult of the Dragon to serve as the main processing hub for wealth stolen from Waterdeep before it is teleported further inland.

## 🗺️ Internal Layout
* **The Courtyard:** Surrounded by wooden stockades, housing the longhouses where the [[Lizardfolk Dissidents]] are kept under watch.
* **The Central Keep:** Contains the administrative quarters of Jos Borngray and the hidden vault areas.
* **The Sub-Levels (Mud Caves):** A flooded network of tunnels controlled by Pharblex Spatterdock's bullywug tribe.
* **The Portal Chamber:** Houses the ancient elven teleportation circle used to transport the hoard instantly to the Greypeak Mountains.
