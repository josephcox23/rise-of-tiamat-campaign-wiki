# Skyreach Cloud-Ship
*A magnificent, soaring vessel carved out of ancient elven timber and reinforced with permanent cloud-sculpting magic.*

> [!abstract] Overview
> - **Propulsion:** Powered by a bound elemental matrix steered by a high-altitude helm chamber.
> - **Layout:** Features a sprawling open upper deck with heavy defensive ballistas, a lower cargo hold containing raided faction treasures, and a roosting platform at the stern.

---

## Tactical Zones

### 1. The Flight Deck & Rigging
* Large, sweeping open spaces susceptible to high wind conditions. 
* Moving along the heavy ropes or climbing the mast structures requires a DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check if the ship is maneuvering sharply.

### 2. The Command Helm
* A pristine chamber at the center of the ship housing the floating cloud-matrix crystal.
* Safe operation requires the pilot to hold a specialized attunement sigil or break the mental block placed on the Cloud Giant pilot.
