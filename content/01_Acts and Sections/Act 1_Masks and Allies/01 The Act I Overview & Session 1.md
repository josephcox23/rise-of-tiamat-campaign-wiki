## The Faction Letters

The players have chosen **Lady [[content/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Laeral Silverhand|Laeral Silverhand]]** as their primary patron, locking in the **[[content/04_Items/Miscellaneous/Silver Warding Tokens|Silver Warding Tokens]]**. However, they are actively hunting down the remaining faction leads along the road.

### 1. The Lords' Alliance (Lady [[content/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Laeral Silverhand|Laeral Silverhand]]) — *PRIMARY*

* **The Angle:** Discretion, statecraft, and tracking the destabilization of the North.
* **The Letter:** *"The tremors you felt are real; our wards are bleeding power. The Cult intends to paralyze [[content/03_Locations/Waterdeep|Waterdeep]] while they secure the Mere of Dead Men. I cannot send an army without triggering a civil panic. Go as ghosts. Take these wards from the palace vaults—let our fading defenses protect you where I cannot send soldiers."*
* **Silver Warding Tokens**

### 2. The Harpers ([[content/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Remallia Haventree|Remallia Haventree]])

* **The Letter:** *"The ledger you recovered spoke of 'Ash Wing' and 'Green Tide' shipments. The cult isn't just marching; they are smuggling supply lines right under our noses. We have an eyes-and-ears agent stationed at a supply outpost along the High Road near the swamp. Find him."*
* **The Seed:** Mentions whispers of *Lyraelis Lament* near Everlund.
* **The Tracked Boon:** Harper Cipher Ring and 2 doses of Dust of Disappearance 

### 3. The Order of the Gauntlet ([[content/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Ontharr Frume|Ontharr Frume]])

* **The Letter:** *"Evil thrives in the dark, but it burns when brought to the light. Sound the call of justice, clear their keep, and shatter their resolve before they can look upon your faces and know fear."*
* **The Seed:** Mentions a cult cell testing a lightning-wreathed bow string connected to *Stormpiercer*.
* **The Tracked Boon:** The Lion’s Battle Horn

* **The Letter:** *"The Mere of Dead Men is a weeping wound, and the cult's presence is turning the wildlife rabid. Respect the wild, or it will become your tomb."*
* **The Seed:** Mentions a dark magical frequency matching the blockades around [[Ithilin Grove]].
* **The Tracked Boon:** Mire-Root Salve (Grants immunity to natural swamp hazards for 4 days).

### 5. The Zhentarim (Rian Nightshade)

* **The Letter:** *"Laeral wants truth, Ontharr wants blood, but I want assets. The cult is moving vast amounts of stolen wealth through that swamp. We have established hidden supply drops along the marsh borders."*
* **The Tracked Boon:** The Black Network Shadow Map (Reveals 3 hidden iron caches along the Mere border).

# Session Summary
::pf
- Having survived an assassination attempt in [[content/03_Locations/Waterdeep|Waterdeep]], the party regrouped in a Lords' Alliance safehouse. They accepted Lady [[content/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Laeral Silverhand|Laeral Silverhand]] as their primary patron, unlocking the Silver Warding Tokens.  
- They received a map of the High Road and faction letters offering competing rewards. The party set out as ghosts to intercept these faction leads while traveling north toward the Mere of Dead Men.
