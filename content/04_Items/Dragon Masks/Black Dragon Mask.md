description: |
  Forged from obsidian and tempered in dragon fire, this black-scaled mask bears the visage of a snarling wyrm. The surface ripples faintly, as if alive and when held close, the carved snout and cheekbones subtly shift to mirror the wearer’s own features, making it appear unsettlingly natural, almost as though it belongs on their face.

  The first time you put it on, the interior feels warm, then cold, then right. 

  Once worn for more than a few minutes, it leaves faint races of soot and black veins at the temples. Those who gaze into your eyes see a faint dark aura, like heat shimmer, dancing in the air around you.

  - - - 
  
  #### Properties (while attuned)
  - +2 Bonus to AC  
  The mask’s hardened scales act as a supernatural barrier.
  - Draconic Sight  
  You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 60 feet).  
  In dim light, faint traces of acidic residue glow green to your eyes, revealing recent draconic or cult activity (functions as a limited Detect Magic focused on acid or draconic effects).
  - Acid Affinity  
  You gain resistance to acid damage.
  - Aura of Dread  
  A faint shadow coils around you, visible in torchlight or moonlight. You have advantage on Intimidation checks, but disadvantage on Persuasion checks with non-evil creatures.
  - Draconic Presence (1/day)  
  As an action, you can exude the mask’s will. Each creature of your choice within 15 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of your next turn.  
  On a success, they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

  ---

  #### Will of the Mask (Curse)
  > The mask is alive with a fragment of Tiamat’s malice. When you first attune to it, make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the mask subtly entwines its will with yours.
  > Each dawn thereafter, roll a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw against the mask’s influence. The effects are cumulative:

  Failed Save 1: 
  - You feel an increasing attachment to the mask. Removing it requires a DC 15 Wisdom save. You become anxious or irritable when it's not in reach.

  Failed Save 2: 
  - You begin hearing faint whispers when near wealth, ruins, or power: Take it. Claim it. It's yours. You must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom save to resist hoarding treasure or powerful objects.

  Failed Save 3: 
  - Your dreams fill with images of black dragons circling over ruins. You gain disadvantage on saving throws against charm or domination effects from dragons or cultists of Tiamat.

  Failed Save 4: 
  - The mask fuses to your skin when worn for more than an hour. Removing it requires Greater Restoration or similar magic. Your eyes become reptilian; you exhale faint acidic vapor when angry.

  Failed Save 5: 
  - You become a thrall of Tiamat's will. The DM may assume control of your character until the curse is broken. Your body begins to transform into a half-draconic form, marked by greed and decay.

  > Removing the mask ends its benefits and its curse, though faint scars or black veins may linger. 

  #### Lore
  - The shifting visage is an intentional design — each replica “adapts” to its wearer to symbolically erase their mortal identity.
  - When two or more of the masks were brought together, they reformed as the Mask of the Dragon Queen, which combined their respective properties and granted them each upon the wearer. 
  - Additional pieces of the five other _dragon masks_ could later be added as well. This valuable artifact was the essential piece needed to complete the ritual that could free Tiamat from the Nine Hells.
