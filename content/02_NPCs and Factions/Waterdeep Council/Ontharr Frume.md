# Ontharr Frume

*A boisterous, muscle-bound paladin of Torm who believes that evil should be met with an open sword and a booming laugh.*

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To crush the Cult of the Dragon in open combat, smash their strongholds, and protect the innocent under the righteous shield of Torm.

## Connections & Relationships

- **The Party:** Adores martial characters (like the Goliath Fighter) and survivalists (like the Ranger). He views the party as champions of light.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** Respects her greatly, but constantly complains that her council meetings involve "too much talking, not enough smiting."
- **The Factions:** Clashes with the Harpers because he views spycraft and deception as dishonorable.
