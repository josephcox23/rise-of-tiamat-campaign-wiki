# The Lords' Alliance

##  Overview

A coalition of rulers from various northern settlements dedicated to maintaining order, security, and economic stability along the Sword Coast. Under the leadership of Open Lord **Lady Laeral Silverhand**, they serve as the primary operational backing for the party's current deployment.
