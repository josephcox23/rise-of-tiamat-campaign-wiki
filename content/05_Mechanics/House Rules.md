### Initiative
- Natural 20: Rolling a Natural 20 on an initiative check allows you to choose exactly where you place yourself in the initiative order, rather than just going first.

### Inspiration
- Earning Inspiration: Exceptional roleplay, staying true to your character, or contributing to the narrative can earn you Inspiration.
- Inspiration Dice: Instead of a simple reroll, Inspiration grants you a bonus die to add to any roll. The DM determines the size of the die awarded depending on the moment (1d4, 1d6, 1d8, 1d10, 1d12, 1d20, or 1d100).
- Stacking & Carryover: These dice stack infinitely and carry over between sessions throughout the duration of the campaign.

### Health Potions
- As an Action: Drinking a healing potion as a full action grants the maximum possible healing for that potion (no rolling required).
- As a Bonus Action: Drinking a healing potion as a bonus action requires you to roll normally for the amount of healing granted.

### Devil’s Bargain
- The Deal: Immediately after any d20 roll is made, a player may request (or the DM may offer) a Devil’s Bargain.
- The Effect: If both the player and the DM agree to the terms, the current roll is instantly changed to either a Natural 1 or a Natural 20, whichever is most advantageous to the character.
- The Catch: In exchange, the DM gains the right to change any single future roll relating to that character to a Natural 1 or a Natural 20 at a time of the DM's choosing.

### Desperate Spellcasting
- Exhausting Resources: When a spellcaster is completely out of spell slots, they may still attempt to cast a spell by sacrificing alternative vital resources instead. They can expend resources such as Hit Dice or automatically mark failed Death Saving Throws to fuel their magic (specific costs to be adjudicated by the DM based on spell level).

### Abstract Wealth System
- To eliminate currency micromanagement and tedious item cost lookups, financial capabilities are tracked using a simplified wealth stat:
- The Score: Every character and settlement has a Wealth Rating ranked from 1 to 5. Items and services are assigned a matching tier based on regional availability, local resources, and the settlement's baseline economy.
- Tier Below Wealth Score (Out-of-Pocket): Any item or service rated below your current Wealth score is a minor, out-of-pocket expense. You can afford it instantly without financial hardship or tracking gold.
- Tier Equal to Wealth Score (Significant Investment): Purchasing an item or service equal to your Wealth score represents a major investment. This incurs immediate financial hardship, temporarily lowering your Wealth score by 1 tier until it can be restored through narrative milestones or roleplay.
- Tier Above Wealth Score (Exorbitant Expense): Any item or service ranked above your current Wealth score is entirely beyond your personal financial means. It cannot be bought outright and must be acquired through alternative methods, such as pooling party resources, completing favors, or narrative roleplay.
