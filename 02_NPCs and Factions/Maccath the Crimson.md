# Maccath the Crimson
*A brilliant, tiefling lore-keeper from the Arcane Brotherhood whose obsession with dragon magic led her straight into a frozen prison.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** She/Her
> - **Race/Age:** Tiefling (Mature Adult)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Speaks in low, exhausted tones, often coughing from the frost-rimed air. She nervously tracks the sound of shifting ice.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To escape her forced servitude under Glazhael and ensure the dragon lore she recovered doesn't fall completely into the hands of the Cult of the Dragon.

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Surrounded by towering stacks of frozen parchments inside an iceberg library, wrapped in heavy crimson furs that are singed at the edges.
* **What She Knows:** 
  - She reveals the locations of the **White Dragon Mask** logistics path leading to the Greypeak Mountains.
  - She passes the party a secret codex detailing how to break the baseline curses on the five original masks.
