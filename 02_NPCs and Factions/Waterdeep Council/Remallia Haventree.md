::pf
# Remallia Haventree
::/pf
::pf
*An elegant, soft-spoken elven noblewoman who leads the Harpers with quiet resolve after a tragic loss.*
::/pf

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** She/Her
> - **Race/Age:** Sun Elf (Adult, around 150 years old)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Soft, rhythmic, and calm. She rarely raises her voice, choosing to lean in closer to speak in meaningful whispers.

::pf
---
::/pf

::pf
## Core Identity
::/pf

::pf
> [!quote] Motivation
> To avenge her husband (who was murdered by the cult) by dismantling the Cult of the Dragon's network using stealth, information, and spycraft.
::/pf

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> Her calm exterior hides deep grief and a quiet fury; she will occasionally support risky, dangerous spy missions if it means dealing a blow to the cult.

## Connections & Relationships
::/pf
::pf
- **The Party:** Values their connection to the streets and their ability to move undetected. She provides them with magical items geared toward stealth.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** A close personal friend, though Remallia believes the Harpers must sometimes operate outside the Open Lord's laws.
- **The Zhentarim:** Deeply distrusts the Black Network, warning the party that any alliance with them will come with a hidden price.

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Usually found in a quiet study or garden, dressed in deep blue robes, gently playing a lute or studying a coded ledger.
* **What they know:** She possesses an intricate web of spies tracking the movements of the Wyrmspeakers and knows the locations of hidden cult portal networks.
* **What they want from the party:** She wants the party to gather intelligence, intercept cult messengers, and use their specific talents (like the Bard's musical skills) to uncover hidden Harper ciphers.
