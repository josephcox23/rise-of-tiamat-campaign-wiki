::pf
# Laeral Silverhand
::/pf
::pf
*The Open Lord of Waterdeep, a powerful and wise wizard carrying the weight of the city on her shoulders.*
::/pf

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** She/Her
> - **Race/Age:** Human (Chosen of Mystra, centuries old but looks like a regal woman in her late 40s)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Clear, commanding, yet warm and deeply encouraging. She speaks with absolute authority but listens to the heroes intently.

::pf
---
::/pf

::pf
## Core Identity
::/pf

::pf
> [!quote] Motivation
> To unite the arguing factions of the Sword Coast into a single, cohesive alliance before the Cult of the Dragon can summon Tiamat and destroy Faerûn.
::/pf

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> She is exhausted by political infighting and secretly fears that the factions will tear each other apart from within before the dragons even arrive.

::pf

::/pf

## Connections & Relationships
::/pf
::pf
- **The Party:** Sees them as true heroes of Waterdeep who can do what armies cannot—act quickly, quietly, and decisively.
- **The Council of Waterdeep:** The nominal leader, constantly managing the bickering delegates from the Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, and other factions.
- **Remallia Haventree:** Respects the Harper delegate's intelligence, though they occasionally clash over clandestine methods.
::/pf

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Surrounded by guards and magical protective circles, she immediately cuts through the room's tension with a welcoming smile for the party.
* **What they know:** She knows the Cult of the Dragon is actively stealing vast hoards of treasure across the Sword Coast and searching for legendary dragon artifacts.
* **What they want from the party:** She wants the party to serve as her elite strike force—investigating cult leads, securing dragon masks, and proving to the other factions that the cult can be defeated.
