::pf
# Ontharr Frume
::/pf
::pf
*A boisterous, muscle-bound paladin of Torm who believes that evil should be met with an open sword and a booming laugh.*
::/pf

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Human (Late 40s, broad-shouldered and heavily armored)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Incredibly loud, deep, and jovial. He slaps shoulders, laughs from his belly, and speaks of honor, glory, and justice constantly.

::pf
---
::/pf

::pf
## Core Identity
::/pf

::pf
> [!quote] Motivation
> To crush the Cult of the Dragon in open combat, smash their strongholds, and protect the innocent under the righteous shield of Torm.
::/pf

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> He is impatient, hates politics, and completely lacks subtlety. He often mistakes reckless bravery for tactical brilliance.

---

::pf
## Connections & Relationships
::/pf
::pf
- **The Party:** Adores martial characters (like the Goliath Fighter) and survivalists (like the Ranger). He views the party as champions of light.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** Respects her greatly, but constantly complains that her council meetings involve "too much talking, not enough smiting."
- **The Factions:** Clashes with the Harpers because he views spycraft and deception as dishonorable.
::/pf

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Can usually be heard before he is seen, either roaring with laughter, drinking ale with his knights, or polishing a massive greatsword.
* **What they know:** He tracks cult battlefield movements, military raiding parties, and has scouts reporting on the physical defenses of cult hideouts.
* **What they want from the party:** He wants the party to take the fight directly to the cult, challenge their champions, and inspire the people of the Sword Coast to stand up and fight.
