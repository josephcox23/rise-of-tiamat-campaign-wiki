# [[Elf-Lord Dralmorrer Borngray]]
*An aristocratic, deeply cynical elf cultist who detests his assignment in the swamp and treats his underlings with casual cruelty.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** High Elf (Adult, looks to be a pristine and elegant noble in his late 100s)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Speaks with a slow, bored, aristocratic drawl. He frequently dabs a perfumed silk handkerchief at his nose to block out the swamp rot, completely ignoring the lower-status creatures around him.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To flawlessly manage the daily logistics of the treasure caravans passing through the swamp, keeping his records pristine so he can secure a swift promotion out of the Mere of Dead Men.

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> He is utterly disgusted by the bullywugs he commands and treats the enslaved lizardfolk like disposable tools. His profound arrogance makes him blind to the growing, volatile mutiny brewing right under his nose.

---

## Connections & Relationships
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]:** His direct superior whom he obeys out of a mix of fear and professional ambition, though her current manic desperation over losing her mask has him deeply on edge.
- **[[06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock]]:** Loathes the bloated bullywug shaman but tolerates him because Rezmir favors the bullywug zealots for internal security.
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw]] & the Lizardfolk:** Views them as mere pack animals, completely unaware that his cruelty has pushed Snapjaw's scouts to the brink of open rebellion.

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Found sitting at a polished mahogany desk that looks entirely out of place in a damp castle tower, fastidiously auditing trade ledgers while sipping fine wine from a silver goblet.
* **What they know:** He knows the exact logistics, schedules, and defensive protocols of the stolen treasure caravans moving through [[Castle Naerytar]], as well as the active bypass codes for the elven teleportation portal below.
* **What they want from the party:** Absolute order and compliance. If the party infiltrates as cultists, he expects them to complete grueling logistical labor without complaint; if they are caught as intruders, he wants them executed quickly and quietly so they don't disrupt his schedule.
- **Story Connection:** [[04 Act I Session 4 The Infiltration of Naerytar (cont.)]]
