# Pharblex Spatterdock
*A bloated, megalomaniacal bullywug shaman who uses his alignment with the cult to violently dominate the local lizardfolk.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Bullywug (Adult)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Speaks with a wet, throaty croak, punctuated by sharp gasps and heavy breathing. He constantly inflates his vocal sac when trying to look intimidating or powerful.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To expand his tribe's dominance over the entire Mere of Dead Men by pleasing [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] and using cult favor to permanently crush the lizardfolk.

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> He is deeply insecure and obsessed with forbidden necromancy. His fanatical religious zealotry makes him blind to the fact that the cult views him as a disposable savage, and his excessive cruelty has pushed his lizardfolk slaves to the bsolute brink of a violent uprising.

---

## Connections & Relationships
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]:** His ultimate patron. He worships her draconic power and obeys her commands implicitly in exchange for her backing his rule over the swamp.
- **Elf-Lord [[Dralmorrer Borngray 1]]:** Deeply dislikes the arrogant elf, but tolerates his presence because Rezmir has given Borngray overall logistical command of the castle.
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw]] & the Lizardfolk:** His hated rivals and slaves. He takes sadistic pleasure in abusing them, enforcing his control through fear, violence, and bullywug taskmasters.

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Found deep within the flooded, mud-choked tunnels beneath the central keep, surrounded by flickering torches, rotting offerings, and swarms of swamp vermin.
* **What they know:** He knows the layout of the flooded cave networks beneath the castle, the patrol schedules of his bullywug zealots, and the basic layout of the upper levels where the cult moves the treasure.
* **What they want from the party:** Total submission or death. If he encounters intruders, he will fiercely protect his domain, viewing the party as a threat to his status and a perfect source of fresh corpses for his necromantic experiments.
- **Story Connection:** [[04 Act I Session 4 The Infiltration of Naerytar (cont.)]]
