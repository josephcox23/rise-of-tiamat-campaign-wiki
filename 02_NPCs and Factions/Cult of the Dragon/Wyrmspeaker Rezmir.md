# Wyrmspeaker Rezmir
*The furious Black Wyrmspeaker, stripped of her legendary mask and hunting the party with relentless, icy vengeance.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** She/Her
> - **Race/Age:** Half-Black Dragon (Adult)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** A low, furious hiss. She moves with terrifying, silent grace, and her reptilian eyes flare with white-hot anger whenever she looks at the heroes who stole from her.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To ambush the party, reclaim the **Black Dragon Mask** they stole from her in Waterdeep, and bring it back to Severin before her failure is discovered.

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> Desperate and humiliated. She hasn't told the other cult leaders that she lost her mask; if she fails to get it back from the party, Severin will destroy her.

---

## Connections & Relationships
- **The Party:** Hates them completely. They didn't just steal a treasure; they stole her status as the Black Wyrmspeaker. She tracks them like prey.
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Severin]]:** Her supreme master. She is terrified of him finding out that a group of mortal adventurers has her mask.
- **[[Captain Borngray]]:** Her elf lieutenant at Castle Naerytar, whom she forces to double patrols to look for "intruders matching the party's description."

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Dressed in dark, tattered robes that smell faintly of swamp rot and sulfur. Her unmasked face reveals a fiercely scarred, reptilian draconic jaw. She glares at the party with pure hatred.
* **What they know:** She knows how the party moves, their fighting styles from Waterdeep, and that whoever is currently attuned to the mask is fighting Tiamat's curse.
* **What they want from the party:** She wants to rip the Black Dragon Mask directly from the party's hands, use Hazirawn to make an example of them, and leave their bones in the swamp.
- **Story Connection:** [[The Infiltration of Naerytar]] | [[The Teleportation Portal & The Mask Clue]]
