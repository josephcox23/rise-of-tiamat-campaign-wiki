# Snapjaw
*A heavily scarred lizardfolk hunter who balances a fierce desire to liberate his people with a deep terror of the cult's retaliation.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Lizardfolk (Adult)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Speaks in broken, hissing Draconic. His movements are calculated and low to the ground, his slitted eyes constantly scanning his surroundings for danger.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> To break the chains of enslavement forced upon his people by [[06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock]]'s bullywug factions and reclaim the independence of the lizardfolk tribes.

> [!warning] Secret / Flaw
> He is terrified of [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]'s wrath Because his people are being brutally punished for the cult's internal failures, he is highly suspicious that outsiders carrying the stolen Black Dragon Mask might just be executioners sent to finish them off.

---

## Connections & Relationships
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir|Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]:** His tyrannical tormentor. He deeply fears her draconic power but despises the cruel collective punishment she is inflicting on his tribe.
- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Pharblex Spatterdock|Pharblex Spatterdock]]:** His direct oppressor. He loathes the bullywug shaman whose forces abuse and enslave the lizardfolk under the cult's direction.
- **The Lizardfolk Scouts:** His loyal hunting party; they look to him for leadership and are ready to mutiny if he gives the word.

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes
* **First Impression:** Crouched low in the humid reeds of a wide marsh clearing, weapons drawn in a tense, waiting stance alongside his hunting party. 
* **What they know:** He knows the exact layout of Castle Naerytar's defenses, the locations of the hidden structural checkpoints, and a secret, partially submerged drainage grate that bypasses the outer boardwalk patrols entirely.
* **What they want from the party:** A reason to hope. He needs a DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check to trust that the party can actually defeat the cult leadership rather than bring more ruin down on his tribe.
- **Story Connection:** [[03 Act I Session 3 Traversing the Mere]]
