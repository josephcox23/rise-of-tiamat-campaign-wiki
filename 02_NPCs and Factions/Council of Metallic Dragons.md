# Council of Metallic Dragons
*An ancient, majestic gathering of the oldest good-aligned dragons on the Sword Coast, meeting in secret to judge whether mortals are worthy of their aid.*

> [!abstract] High Council Seats
> - **Protanther (Gold):** Stern, ancient, deeply distrustful of humanoids due to past histories of greed and betrayal.
> - **Otaerhigi (Silver):** Empathetic to the mortal plight, willing to listen to the heroes' achievements.
> - **Ileuthra (Brass):** Fascinated by stories, puzzles, and tactical accomplishments.

---

## Faction Standing & Boons

### Requirements for Alliance
The boys must present their captured true Dragon Masks (Black, White, Green) as concrete proof that they are actively destroying the cult's baseline power structure.

### Faction Rewards (Unlocked)
* **Ancestral Magic Weapons:** Legendary gear pulled from ancient hoards specifically tailored to combat dragon threats.
* **The Silver Flight:** Direct aerial reinforcement from young metallic dragons during the siege on the Well of Dragons.
