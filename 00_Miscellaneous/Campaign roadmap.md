# Summary 
### Prologue: Waterdeep Dragon Heist
- [x] The Starting Point: The boys reuse their Level 8 characters from Lost Mine of Phandelver. Instead of running the full political/downtime-heavy version of Dragon Heist, it serves as a fast-paced 1–2 session springboard in Waterdeep.
- [x] The Big Twist & Reward: During this quick heist/prologue, the boys successfully intercept and capture the Black Dragon Mask early.
### Rise of Tiamat campaign:
#### The Core Adjustment: The Hunted Heroes
 - Rezmir’s Grudge: Because the party have the Black Dragon Mask, Wyrmspeaker Rezmir doesn't have it safely secured in a castle. Instead, she is completely obsessed, furious, and actively hunting the party down across the Sword Coast to rip it back from them. This turns her into an immediate, recurring, and terrifying nemesis tailored to keep the kids highly engaged.

 - The Curse of the Mask: The Black Dragon Mask isn't just a trophy; it is alive with a fragment of Tiamat's malice. Whoever holds or attunes to it must resist its progressive curse ("Will of the Mask"), which whispers to them, encourages hoarding treasure, and slowly attempts to turn them into a thrall of Tiamat.

#### The Campaign Flow (Streamlined for Kids)
Rather than focusing heavily on complex political V faction bickering during the Council of Waterdeep, the campaign is reshaped into an action-forward loop:

>- The Council Support: High-profile figures like Laeral Silverhand act as clear allies, directing the boys and giving them a grand sense of importance as champions of the Sword Coast.
>- The Strike Missions: The Council sends the characters out on high-excitement missions to dismantle the Cult's leadership, track down the remaining four Dragon Masks, and stop cult raids.
>- The Cult Strikes Back: Because the boys possess a true mask and are actively ruining the Cult's plans, the Cult coordinates escalating ambushes and assassination attempts specifically targeting them.

#### Rallying the Ultimate Army
To prepare for the final showdown, the boys embark on epic, kid-pleasing diplomatic missions:

 >- The Metallic Dragons: They travel to negotiate directly with ancient, good-aligned metallic dragons (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Brass, Copper) to secure aerial reinforcement.
 >- Undead Alliances: They venture into Thay to deal with a splinter faction of Red Wizards, weakening the spellcasting backing of the Cult.

#### The Grand Finale: The Well of Dragons
The campaign concludes with an epic, video-game-style apocalyptic war:

 >- While the metallic dragons and faction armies fight the cultists and chromatic dragons outside, the boys infiltrate the caldera stronghold.
 >- Disrupting the Ritual: They enter the temple to stop the Red Wizards from using massive hoards of gold to summon Tiamat.
 >- The Final Battle: Because the boys already took the Black Dragon Mask at the very beginning of their journey, the Cult's ritual is permanently compromised. This ensures Tiamat emerges in a significantly weakened state, giving your boys the perfect, heroic opportunity to defeat the dragon goddess and banish her back to the Nine Hells!
 
 





# Session Outlines
### Act I: The Flight into the Mere of Dead Men
The Goal: Flee Waterdeep with the stolen Black Dragon Mask, gather allies along the dangerous High Road, and infiltrate the cult's swamp stronghold before Rezmir's hunters catch up.

#### Session 1 – The Morning After & Faction Mobilization
- Having survived an assassination attempt in Waterdeep, the party regrouped in a Lords' Alliance safehouse. They accepted Lady Laeral Silverhand as their primary patron, unlocking the Silver Warding Tokens.  
- They received a map of the High Road and faction letters offering competing rewards. The party set out as ghosts to intercept these faction leads while traveling north toward the Mere of Dead Men.  

#### Session 2 – The High Road 
- The before the entrance to the Mere of Dead Men, the party came across a chasm with a raging river at the bottom. Across the span, there was a guard tower with a rope bridge connecting the two sides. The tower was guarded by members of the Cult of the Dragon which the party dispatched of.
- Following the encounter the party discovered a lockbox containing a clue to the Verdant Fang.
 - [x] The Weapon Seed: Logan (the Druid) received a powerful vision of a shattered emerald spear buried in the roots of a banyan tree—the Verdant Fang is waiting for him in the swamp.  

#### Session 3 – The Sunken Gate & Camp Naerytar Approaches
- The Action: On the road, the party seeks their faction boons:
    - [ ] The Harpers: Swapped tunes with a bard agent to gain a Cipher Ring and Dust of Disappearance.  
    - [ ] The Order of the Gauntlet: Swore an oath to fleeing clerics to claim The Lion's Battle Horn.  
    - [ ] The Zhentarim: Scaled a wet rock face mid-combat against a Cult Blockade to grab a hidden cache.  
 - The Climax: They reached the swamp and solved the Sunken Gate Puzzle. By clearing the Pillar of Teeth, purging the Pillar of Rot, and singing to the Pillar of Word, they opened the path.  
 - The Setup: The boys navigate the sulfurous inner marsh of the Mere of Dead Men using their Emerald Enclave maps/salves or tracking the remaining two Zhentarim poison caches.  
* **The Encounter (Snapjaw’s War Band):** Upon solving the gate, they are ambushed by [[02_NPCs and Factions/Cult of the Dragon/Snapjaw|Snapjaw]] and his lizardfolk hunting party. Snapjaw notices the true Black Dragon Mask the boys captured in Waterdeep. 
* **The Choice:** The boys roll Persuasion/Intimidation using their draconic leverage. 
 > **Success:** Snapjaw reveals a furious Rezmir is tearing Castle Naerytar apart looking for them. He offers a secret western drainage grate entrance to launch a surprise attack.
> **Failure:** The lizardfolk retreat to warn the castle, putting the stronghold on high alert.

#### Session 4 – The Infiltration of Naerytar
- The Infiltration: The boys breach the castle (either via Snapjaw's unlatched grate or fighting through a reinforced front gate).  
- The Powder Keg: They enter a three-way political nightmare between the ruling Cultists, the cruel Bullywugs, and the enslaved Lizardfolk. The boys can trigger a massive lizardfolk rebellion by taking out the bullywug shaman, Pharblex Spatterdock, in the mud caves.  
- The Bosses: They must clear out Pharblex (grabbing his nature spellbook and crocodile shield) and defeat Elf-Lord Borngray in the command tower (securing 1,500 gp and portal maps).  
- The Cliffhanger:  In the pristine marble cellars, they find an active, glowing elven teleportation circle. They loot Borngray’s desk for Rezmir’s abandoned ledger, discovering that she just fled through the circle. The ledger reveals the portal's activation password: *"Drako."*

## Act II: The Frozen Chase & The White Mask

### Session 5 – The Council of Waterdeep & The Mountain Lodge
* **The Return:** Stepping through the teleportation circle, the boys find themselves in a remote portal hub and use their contacts to return seamlessly to Waterdeep.
* **The Second Council:** The boys present their findings to Lady Laeral Silverhand. Stunned by their success, the Council tasks them with tracking down the **[[White Dragon Mask|White]]** and **[[Green Dragon Mask|Green]]** masks.
* **The Frozen Leads:** Lorekeeper [[Maccath the Crimson]] reveals clues pointing north to the Sea of Moving Ice. The boys travel there, tracking a cult logistics line, and discover the terrifying iceberg lair of the ancient white dragon, **[[Glazhael the Cloudchaser]]**.
* **The White Dragon Fight:** The boys confront Glazhael in his frost-rimed cave. The dragon is an absolute powerhouse, forcing them to fight tooth and nail. They drive him off or force him to flee to the mountains.
* **The Hunting Lodge (The White Mask Vault):** Following Glazhael's flight path to a luxurious, snow-covered hunting lodge in the Greypeak Mountains, they confront **[[Talis the White]]**. Unlike the original module, **the real White Dragon Mask is physically here** inside her frozen vault!
* **The Nemesis Intrudes:** Just as they claim the mask, **[[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]** arrives with a specialized tracking squad. She ambushes the party, trying to rip back the Black Mask. Rezmir uses *Hazirawn* to deal heavy damage but activates a teleportation contingency ring to escape right before the boys can strike a killing blow, leaving them with **two true masks** (Black & White).

## Act III: The Skies of the Misty Forest & The Green Mask

### Session 6 – The Skyreach Cloud-Ship Heist
* **The Setup:** The boys follow the trail of the **[[Green Dragon Mask]]** to the Misty Forest, only to find the cult loading their spoils onto **[[Skyreach]]**, a colossal flying elven cloud-ship powered by a Cloud Giant pilot.
* **The Chase:** The cloud-ship begins lifting off into the storm clouds. The boys must execute a breathless, high-stakes skill challenge or aerial wyvern-mount heist to board the ascending vessel before it climbs out of reach.
* **The Elven Traitor:** Once on board, they discover the ship is commanded by **King Melandrach’s own rogue son-{son’s name}**, who has turned traitor and become the Green Wyrmspeaker to save his own skin. He controls the adult green dragon, **[[Chuth]]**, who nests on the top deck.
* **The Sky Battle:** A chaotic multi-level fight across the rigging and open decks against the elven traitor and Chuth. Upon victory, the boys claim the **[[Green Dragon Mask]]** and take control of the cloud-ship as their mobile base of operations.

## Act IV: Dragon Politics & The Red & Blue Masks

### Session 7 – The Metallic Council & The Search
* **The Grand Arrival:** The boys sail their captured cloud-ship directly over Waterdeep, arriving as absolute legendary heroes.
* **The Metallic Council:** Because they hold three true masks, the Council of Waterdeep sends them as supreme mortal delegates to the Networld Mountains to negotiate with the proud, ancient **[[Council of Metallic Dragons]]** (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Brass). 
* **The Reward:** The boys successfully win the dragons' respect through roleplay and their trophy masks, unlocking the locations to their **Ancestral Magic Weapons** and a dragon army.
* **The Next Lead:** The dragon council reveals the locations of the final two artifacts: the **[[Red Dragon Mask|Red]]** and **[[Blue Dragon Mask|Blue]]** masks.

### Sessions 8 & 9 – The Red & Blue Mask Strikes
* *Note: Keep these sessions focused on fast, action-packed dungeon delves tailored to your players' favorite combat styles.*
* **Session 8 (The Blue Mask):** An infiltration into a lightning-shattered desert canyon stronghold, overcoming electric traps to secure the **[[Blue Dragon Mask]]**.
* **Session 9 (The Red Mask):** A high-temperature strike into a fiery volcanic cavern, defeating a Red Wizard archmage **{name}** to rip away the **[[Red Dragon Mask]]**. The party now have **all five original Dragon Masks**!

## Act V: The Caldera Grand Finale

### Session 10 – The Well of Dragons
- Final Council of Waterdeep, battle preparations
	- Skill challenges for placement of forces
* **The War Arc:** Outside the massive volcanic caldera of the Well of Dragons, the grand faction armies and the metallic dragons engage in a massive war against chromatic dragons and cult legions.
* **The Infiltration:** The boys dive straight into the central subterranean temple.
* **The Mid-Boss (Severin's Last Stand):** They confront Supreme Leader **[[Severin]]** at the center of the ritual platform. He fights like a desperate fanatic. The boys defeat him, but with his absolute dying breath, Severin screams a final blood sacrifice, slamming his hands into the altar.
* **The Volatile Summoning:** Because the boys captured **all five original masks**, the ritual is totally compromised. Severin's sacrifice triggers the ritual anyway using **crude, cracking replica masks** (the weaker training versions). The portal rips open, unstable and burning with purple flame.
* **The Tiamat Confrontation (The Weakened Boss Mechanics):**
  [[Tiamat]] forces her way through the broken portal, but her form is visibly cracked, unstable, and missing her legendary immunities. To make the fight highly interactive for the kids rather than a boring health-bar slog, incorporate the following stage challenges:
### Tiamat battle:
#### STAGE 1: THE REPLICA SHATTER (Heads 1 & 2)
- Tiamat emerges with only her Blue and White heads fully formed. The other three heads are shifting, ghostly energy.
- Challenge: The boys must strike the cracking replica masks floating around the ritual pillars. Every time a replica mask is shattered by a player's attack or spell, one of Tiamat's active heads loses its reaction/legendary action for a round.

#### STAGE 2: THE PORTAL ANCHORS (Heads 3 & 4)
- As Tiamat pulls her massive body further out, the Red and Green heads manifest, breathing fire and poison. 
- Challenge: Crimson planar chains anchor her to the mortal realm. Andy (Ranger) or Robert (Fighter) can use their heavy weapons/actions to break the physical stone anchors around the room, which directly deals massive damage to Tiamat and inflicts a conditional vulnerability (e.g., shattering the Earth Anchor makes her vulnerable to Logan's druid spellcasting for a turn).

#### STAGE 3: THE FIVE-HEADED BANISHMENT (The Final Blow)
- The 
Black head fully manifests as she stands completely in the caldera, preparing a cataclysmic multi-breath weapon.
- Challenge: The boys must use their true Dragon Masks or Ancestral Weapons simultaneously. Channeling their heroic willpower as a coordinated team activity completely destabilizes her remaining replica magic, shattering her back into the Nine Hells in a brilliant flash of light.
